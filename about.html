"We started with a simple belief: clean energy should be accessible, reliable, and built to last."

Founded in the vibrant city of Jaipur, Rajasthan, Doon Energy Solutions emerged from a vision to revolutionize the energy landscape in India. We recognized early on that transitioning to renewable energy wasn't just an ecological imperative, but a profound economic opportunity for businesses and homeowners alike.

Our journey began with a relentless focus on Solar EPC—designing, procuring, and constructing robust solar power plants. As the industry evolved, so did we. We expanded into the cutting-edge domain of LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) Battery Manufacturing, positioning ourselves as an end-to-end provider of energy generation and storage solutions.

Today, our commitment to sustainability, innovation, and client partnership drives every project we undertake, pushing boundaries and setting new benchmarks in the renewable energy sector.
