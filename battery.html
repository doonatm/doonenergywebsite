Why Lithium LFP?

The Safest, Longest-lasting Battery Chemistry

Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) is the premier choice for stationary storage, offering unmatched safety and cycle life compared to traditional battery technologies.

Thermal Safety

Inherently stable chemistry with no risk of thermal runaway, making it the safest lithium technology available.

High Cycle Life

Delivers 4000+ cycles at 80% Depth of Discharge, lasting up to 10 years even with daily use.

Wide Temperature Range

Performs reliably in extreme Indian climates, operating smoothly from -20°C to 60°C.

Eco-friendly

100% recyclable and free from toxic heavy metals like lead or cobalt.
