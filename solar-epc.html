What We Do

Engineering · Procurement · Construction

We handle every aspect of your solar project with precision and expertise, delivering a seamless transition to clean energy.

Engineering

  • Site survey & shadow analysis
  • Load analysis & capacity sizing
  • Structural design & wind load calculations
  • Electrical single-line diagrams (SLD)
  • 3D modeling & layout planning

Procurement

  • Tier-1 panels (Adani, Waaree, Vikram)
  • High-efficiency string inverters
  • Galvanized iron (GI) mounting structures
  • Premium DC/AC cables & conduits
  • Advanced protection devices (ACDB/DCDB)

Construction

  • Civil works & foundation laying
  • Mounting structure installation
  • Precise panel placement & alignment
  • Electrical wiring & inverter commissioning
  • Grid tie-in, testing & handover
